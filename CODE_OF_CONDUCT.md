# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

EmoLens is built for neurodivergent youth and the people who support them. We hold contributors to that same standard of care: this is a harassment-free, inclusive space for everyone, regardless of age, disability, neurotype, gender identity, sexual orientation, race, religion, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and code review
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before escalating
- Use welcoming, inclusive language — avoid ableist or dismissive terms
- Respect differing communication styles and processing speeds

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Dismissing neurodivergent perspectives or lived experience

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainer. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this code of conduct.
